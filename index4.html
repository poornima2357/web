About Our School

Quality Education With Strong Values

EAB Govt Higher Secondary School is dedicated to academic excellence, discipline and overall student development. Our institution provides a positive environment where students gain knowledge, creativity and leadership skills.

The school offers modern laboratories, computer facilities, sports infrastructure and an enriched library to support holistic education.
