About Our School

Shaping the Future of
Students

EAB Government Higher Secondary School is a state-run institution nestled in the serene hills of Thenponmudi, Kumaran Kundru, Coimbatore District. We proudly serve as the premier High School for the region, offering Classes VI through XII under the Tamil Nadu Directorate of School Education.

Our experienced faculty blends proven pedagogy with modern approaches — nurturing academic excellence, moral character, and cultural pride across all seven high school grades.

  • Tamil Nadu State Board (TNBSE) affiliated — Classes VI to XII
  • High School: Middle (VI–VIII), Secondary (IX–X), Higher Secondary (XI–XII)
  • Science, Arts & Commerce streams available in Class XI & XII
  • Government scholarships, free uniform, books, and mid-day meals
  • Safe, inclusive, child-friendly hill-station campus environment
